# Ars Dialectica verification experiment

**Question:** Can the foundation distinguish construction, independent proof
verification, and conformity to Will without confusing an open proposition with a
`Potential` Effect?

The producer applies two declared modus-ponens instructions and emits an `Effect`
with a `Demonstration`. A separate module checks the premises, every application,
the reported conclusion, and the Effect boundary. A second, terminating check
compares the verified Effect with Will under equality declared by Ars.

## Assumptions

- Finite atoms, implication, and modus ponens are a probe, not a general logic.
- Structural proposition equality and the exact-proposition Will fragment belong
  only to this experimental Ars.
- `Refused`, `Failed`, invalid proof, and nonconformity are competing outcome
  hypotheses; they are not accepted language categories.
- `Open` means neither proved nor refuted. It does not carry a family of possible
  Effects and is therefore deliberately not represented as `Potential`.

## Success signal

- Spell capabilities are derived by elaboration rather than authored as a second
  list.
- A permitted two-step Cast creates a new Effect and an inspectable trace.
- The independent verifier accepts the trace and a separate Will check conforms.
- A valid Effect may nevertheless fail its Will comparison.
- Missing premises fail after permission; missing permission refuses before work.
- Corrupting a proof is detected even when the producer is not rerun.

Run with:

```console
python -m experiments.ars_dialectica_verification
python -m pytest tests/experiments/test_ars_dialectica_verification.py
```

## Deletion or promotion path

Delete the probe if independent verification produces no observable distinction.
Promote only distinctions that survive the cross-domain synthesis gate; do not
promote this proposition representation or outcome taxonomy by itself.
