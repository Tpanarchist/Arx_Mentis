# Foundation evidence ledger

**Status:** Provisional evidence record. This is not a semantic specification.

Each experimental domain counts as one source of evidence regardless of its number
of witnesses or tests. `Supports` means the experiment exhibited the behavior;
`boundary` means it also exercised a negative or counterexample case; `conflicts`
means an experiment embodied a competing model; and `not exercised` contributes no
evidence.

## Euclid I.1 baseline

### Observed

- Given Forms remain immutable and Cast produces a new Effect.
- Required capabilities are derived by elaborating exact steps under an Ars.
- The initial Ars refuses the unchanged Spell at circle intersection after a
  two-step elaborated prefix; the extended Ars permits it.
- The two intersection results form an inspectable equivariant family, and the
  remaining Lines and triangle are constructed uniformly through it.
- Direct, twisted, and independent frame composition have respectively two, two,
  and four joint results with observably different correlations.
- An invariant equilateral Will conforms without settling the triangle; a
  side-specific Will produces a nonconstant truth family.
- Equality evidence is qualified by an Ars and the length proof crosses an explicit
  Bridge with preservation, commutation, and equivariance obligations.
- Fixedness and constructibility are separately necessary for a legitimate default.
- Every construction step and conformity proof has inspectable provenance.

### Assumed by the disposable representation

- A finite symbolic reflection group adequately isolates the relevant symmetry.
- Python frozen dataclasses, frozen sets, and explicit family graphs are suitable
  experimental carriers.
- Lines are stored as endpoint pairs while direction and chirality are modeled by a
  derived frame-placement report.
- Declared equivariance witnesses stand in for machine-checked general proofs.

### Not established

- A general geometry, general proof calculus, or complete Will language.
- How equivariant families or torsors appear in source notation or runtime storage.
- General Potential resumption, continuation branching, or settlement failure
  semantics.
- Whether Spell is fundamentally nominal or only a contextual reading role.
- A general Reader protocol or composition law among Reader types.
- That the proposed Cast outcome types belong in the language rather than the
  experimental harness.
- That the chosen ambient transformation group is adequate beyond this proposition.

## Synthesis gate

A distinction becomes an ADR candidate only when it:

1. performs the same semantic job in at least three unrelated experimental domains;
2. has at least one negative or counterexample witness at its boundary;
3. can be stated without a shared Python representation; and
4. has backend-neutral observable behavior.

The matrix admits five related candidates: derived capabilities, explicit permission
refusal, persistent source/new result, production separate from Will comparison, and
the Refusal/Failure distinction. Ars-qualified equality also reaches the numerical
threshold, but its laws and Bridge obligations remain underspecified outside
Geometrica. Candidate status authorizes an ADR discussion; it does not accept a
language category or implementation type.

The foundation gate as a whole remains **closed**. Its central Potential algebra has
only two independent domains, and full Ars declarations, Bridges, default
legitimacy, typed Reader composition, and independent proof verification have fewer
than three. Therefore this phase produces no tokens, grammar, AST, types, runtime
values, shared foundation classes, or executable source notation.

## Recorded unresolved Potentials

- nominal Spell versus Spell solely as a contextual role;
- equivariant candidate-family values versus any model requiring complete Effects
  at the family boundary;
- endpoint-pair Line versus separately oriented Line;
- exact language-level names and payloads for Effect, Refusal, Failure, invalid
  Demonstration, Counterexample, and Open;
- universal Ars record versus domain-specific declarations with explicit omissions;
- non-invariant Will rejection versus a continuation explicitly lifted through a
  Potential truth family.
