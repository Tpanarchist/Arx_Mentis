# Foundation evidence ledger

**Status:** Provisional evidence record. This is not a semantic specification.

Each experimental domain counts as one source of evidence regardless of its number
of witnesses or tests. `Supports` means the experiment exhibited the behavior;
`boundary` means it also exercised a negative or counterexample case; `conflicts`
means an experiment embodied a competing model; and `not exercised` contributes no
evidence.

## Euclid I.1 baseline

### Observed

- Given Forms remain immutable and Cast produces a new Effect.
- Required capabilities are derived by elaborating exact steps under an Ars.
- The initial Ars refuses the unchanged Spell at circle intersection after a
  two-step elaborated prefix; the extended Ars permits it.
- The two intersection results form an inspectable equivariant family, and the
  remaining Lines and triangle are constructed uniformly through it.
- Direct, twisted, and independent frame composition have respectively two, two,
  and four joint results with observably different correlations.
- An invariant equilateral Will conforms without settling the triangle; a
  side-specific Will produces a nonconstant truth family.
- Equality evidence is qualified by an Ars and the length proof crosses an explicit
  Bridge with preservation, commutation, and equivariance obligations.
- Fixedness and constructibility are separately necessary for a legitimate default.
- Every construction step and conformity proof has inspectable provenance.

### Assumed by the disposable representation

- A finite symbolic reflection group adequately isolates the relevant symmetry.
- Python frozen dataclasses, frozen sets, and explicit family graphs are suitable
  experimental carriers.
- Lines are stored as endpoint pairs while direction and chirality are modeled by a
  derived frame-placement report.
- Declared equivariance witnesses stand in for machine-checked general proofs.

### Not established

- A general geometry, general proof calculus, or complete Will language.
- How equivariant families or torsors appear in source notation or runtime storage.
- General Potential resumption, continuation branching, or settlement failure
  semantics.
- Whether Spell is fundamentally nominal or only a contextual reading role.
- A general Reader protocol or composition law among Reader types.
- That the proposed Cast outcome types belong in the language rather than the
  experimental harness.
- That the chosen ambient transformation group is adequate beyond this proposition.

## State Zero Experiment 005 baseline

### Observed

- Neutral three-channel mediation executes without foundation-named classes or
  imports from the package or another experiment.
- The cyclic action is free and transitive, and channel-dependent provenance changes
  equivariantly with each definite trace.
- Shared, one-step-twisted, and independent compositions expose three, three, and
  nine joint results. A naive option bag reduces all three to the same local view.
- Aggregate transfer maps uniformly to a constant family and can be exposed as one
  public result while retaining the unresolved mediation family.
- Signature construction maps uniformly to a nonconstant three-way family.
- Later evidence identifies shared and twisted correlations, does not identify an
  independent partner, preserves identity under irrelevant or insufficient
  observations, and returns owned conflict or rejection results at its boundaries.
- Endpoint acceptance succeeds without channel identification; channel-specific
  acceptance remains a nonconstant truth family.
- No individual channel is fixed by the cyclic action or justified by list position.

### Assumed by the disposable representation

- Three symbolic channels and equal integer transfer isolate the mediation mechanics
  without making a physical or occult claim.
- Frozen graphs, enums, and operation-specific provenance are experimental carriers.
- Signature observations are sufficient and trustworthy within the declared neutral
  observation law.

### Not established

- That virtual mediation describes physics, omens, or conjuration.
- That indirect observability is a language-level type dimension.
- Whether a public outcome and unresolved internal mechanism inhabit one Effect,
  related Effects, or a different construction record.
- Whether identifying a channel changes semantic state or only adds knowledge.
- Any accepted correspondence between neutral Intervention, Trace, Outcome, or
  Observation and foundation vocabulary.

## State Zero Experiment 006 baseline

### Observed

- A RecordedAim precedes every event, while event occurrence, rule-governed
  correspondence, apparent surprise, and causal attribution remain separate records.
- Ordinary chance produces a real match without support for anomalous mediation.
- Selective reporting changes the apparent match rate from 1/6 to 1/1 without
  changing the event stream or its generator.
- Operator attention and behavior form an inspectable causal chain to a matching
  event, while the same public event can occur under four different definite causes.
- Relevant evidence changes unequal support scores; irrelevant evidence is inert and
  contradictory evidence is explicit.
- Blocking operator behavior changes support among hypotheses while five explanations
  remain live rather than selecting one.
- A forced six-way cycle is transitive only by changing causal variables, evidence
  requirements, or prior support. The structure-preserving relation has six
  disconnected orbits.
- One world cause remains definite but unavailable in the observer record, whose
  causal attribution is underdetermined even though the event is settled.

### Assumed by the disposable representation

- Declared deterministic streams and intervention summaries stand in for empirical
  sampling; this experiment makes no statistical or causal claim about the world.
- Integer support scores witness inequality but are neither probabilities nor
  proposed semantics.
- Exact symbolic MatchRules and a privileged world record are experimental carriers.

### Not established

- A general theory of causality, probability, evidence, attribution, or omens.
- Final names or relations among Potential, Uncertainty, Open, Chance, and Search.
- Whether knowledge state belongs to Context, Reader, Effect, or another construct.
- That every non-symmetric unresolved state is epistemic rather than objective.
- Any package representation, source notation, or accepted foundation mapping.

## State Zero Experiment 007 baseline

### Observed

- A TargetForm is recorded before execution and remains immutable while every
  permitted Transition produces a separately owned WorldState.
- Criterion-only execution uses the same baseline choices for targets with different
  guidance preferences; the target is consulted only during later acceptance.
- Policy guidance changes the selected path and retains an inspectable CausalTrace.
  Sustained control consults target-relative Feedback, while a released compiled
  policy continues without target availability.
- An explicit hidden weighting provides a third causal mechanism. Criterion-only,
  policy-guided, and hidden-weighted runs can produce the same accepted WorldState
  while retaining distinct traces.
- Directly assigning the target state is rejected as circular because it supplies no
  Transition or causal account.
- Targets with identical AcceptanceRules derive different ActionPolicies, while
  targets with different acceptance thresholds derive the same transition ordering.
- Removing the target-to-policy channel restores the baseline path. Blocking all
  starting transitions makes the target unreachable, and blocking a later
  transition exposes partial progress without reporting conformity.
- Outcome-only observer attribution leaves three mechanisms live even though the
  experimental world record retains one definite trace.

### Assumed by the disposable representation

- A finite deterministic transition graph adequately isolates criterion, guidance,
  feedback, constraints, and attribution without making a physical or occult claim.
- Frozen Python records, declared rankings, and integer weights are experimental
  carriers rather than proposed language values or causal laws.
- The CausalTrace is privileged world evidence; an outcome-only observer is not
  entitled to infer it.

### Not established

- Whether Will names only AcceptanceRule, also names TargetForm, or participates in
  deriving construction guidance.
- A general theory of control, approximation, causation, planning, or goal pursuit.
- That a compiled policy may outlive its source in the eventual ownership model.
- Any accepted mapping from TargetForm, ActionPolicy, Controller, Feedback, Outcome,
  or CausalTrace to foundation categories.
- Any package representation, source notation, external action, or physical claim.

## Synthesis gate

A distinction becomes an ADR candidate only when it:

1. performs the same semantic job in at least three unrelated experimental domains;
2. has at least one negative or counterexample witness at its boundary;
3. can be stated without a shared Python representation; and
4. has backend-neutral observable behavior.

The earlier execution distinctions remain candidates. ADR 0008 now accepts five
narrow, backend-neutral obligations for unresolved constructions whose alternatives
are related by declared symmetry: equivariance, correlation preservation, invariant
descent, evidence-driven settlement, and historical persistence. Experiment 006
prevents that decision from becoming a universal algebra of unresolvedness. The ADR
does not accept a carrier, nominal Potential construct, syntax, AST, runtime value,
or shared implementation. Ars-qualified equality also reaches the numerical
threshold, but its laws and Bridge obligations remain underspecified outside
Geometrica.

Experiment 007 reinforces the separation of production from acceptance and adds a
direct counterexample to identifying acceptance with guidance. Its stronger result
is still only one domain: one neutral TargetForm can derive separate AcceptanceRule
and ActionPolicy records. That is pressure for contextual roles, not evidence that
foundation Will must absorb guidance or that those Python records should be
promoted.

The foundation gate as a whole remains **closed**. Accepting ADR 0008 does not satisfy
the independent gaps in full Ars declarations, Bridges, default legitimacy, typed
Reader composition, independent proof verification, or the identity and derivation
roles of Will. Therefore this phase produces no tokens, grammar, AST, types, runtime
values, shared foundation classes, or executable source notation.

## Recorded unresolved questions

- nominal Spell versus Spell solely as a contextual role;
- equivariant candidate-family values versus any model requiring complete Effects
  at the family boundary;
- endpoint-pair Line versus separately oriented Line;
- exact language-level names and payloads for Effect, Refusal, Failure, invalid
  Demonstration, Counterexample, and Open;
- universal Ars record versus domain-specific declarations with explicit omissions;
- non-invariant Will rejection versus a continuation explicitly lifted through a
  Potential truth family;
- public Effect containing, relating to, or merely exposing an unresolved internal
  mechanism;
- observability, availability, resolution, verification, and construction success as
  separate dimensions rather than one vague unknown state;
- symmetric Potential versus unequal epistemic support, probabilistic Chance, open
  proof, and unfinished Search;
- definite world state versus Reader-relative knowledge about that state;
- Will solely as an acceptance predicate versus TargetForm also supplying a distinct
  guidance reading;
- sustained target-relative Feedback versus a released compiled policy, including
  its source-availability and provenance rules;
- partial progress and approximation versus terminating Conformity;
- actual CausalTrace versus observer attribution from an indistinguishable outcome.

## Next evidence

Keep accepted ADR 0008 scoped to unresolved constructions with declared symmetry and
do not select a Python carrier. Test the Experiment 007 role split in unrelated
domains before changing Will: acceptance and guidance must remain independently
observable even if one source Form can derive both. Continue investigating unequal
epistemic support and partial narrowing separately from Potential, then target Bridge
laws, complete Ars declarations, typed Reader composition, default legitimacy, and
independent proof verification. The full gate remains closed to source notation.
