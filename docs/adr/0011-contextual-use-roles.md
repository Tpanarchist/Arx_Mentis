# ADR 0011: Explicit reading is distinct from execution authority

Status: Accepted

## Context

Four independent domains exhibit one carrier or source under multiple lawful roles:

- Ars Grammatica reads one generic Form for inspection or execution; changing the
  explicit Reader role changes behavior without mutating the Form.
- Symbolic Release reads one keyed Carrier into policy or acceptance artifacts;
  unsupported readings return owned results and the Carrier does not interpret
  itself.
- Scoped Model Adoption derives a PredictionRule or operative DerivedPolicy from the
  same Model under explicit predictive or control Adoption. Crossed and unsupported
  uses are refused, and Model contains neither role intrinsically.
- Bound Delegation reads one compiled Delegate for Audit or Control. Audit remains
  lawful for an instructions-only copy, while Control execution separately requires
  admitted lineage, Activation, authority, capability, scope, lease, and budget.

The experiments share no carrier representation. Their common observable job is to
make role selection explicit while preserving source identity and rejecting
unsupported readings.

## Decision

> One settled structure may support multiple declared operational readings. The
> selected reading must be explicit and lawful, and choosing a reading does not
> itself confer the authority required to perform it.

This accepts role multiplicity and the role/authority separation only. Interpretation
may expose meaningful operations while Context, capability, or another explicit grant
still refuses their execution.

## Boundary

This is not the claim that every role is contextual or that nominal roles are
forbidden. Euclid, Astronomica, and Dialectica still use nominal Spell
representations. The decision does not settle whether foundation Spell is nominal,
contextual, or a combination.

## Explicitly not decided

- representation of roles, Readers, keys, Adoptions, or artifacts;
- role composition, subtyping, capabilities, or authorization;
- whether interpretation and activation are one operation;
- syntax, AST, types, runtime values, diagnostics, or package API.

## Implementation deferral

Bound Delegation supplies the missing hostile authority boundary without importing a
shared carrier. The accepted law still authorizes no Reader type, dispatch mechanism,
capability representation, syntax, runtime value, or package feature. Nominal Spell
counterexamples and role-composition laws remain unresolved behind the full gate.
